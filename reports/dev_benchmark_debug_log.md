## Confirmed Findings vs. Hypotheses

### Confirmed Findings (Supported Directly by Data):
1. **Archive Retrieval Efficacy:** In 3 out of the 4 answerable questions (Q1, Q4, Q5), the supporting fact had been placed into `archive_store` by lifecycle management to respect the 500-token active memory cap. Under the archive-retrieval fix, all 3 facts were retrieved at **Rank 1**, directly confirming the value of allowing valid archived facts to compete for retrieval.
2. **Proper Unannotated Question Handling:** Question 2 (`bc8a6e93_abs`) has 0 ground truth supporting evidence strings. The evaluation harness correctly output `NaN` for Recall@K, Precision@K, and MRR, preventing metric distortion.
3. **Unanchored Slot Affinity Distorts Ranking in Q3:** In `compute_query_relevance`, a memory with `slot_type: location` was awarded `+0.40` boost simply because the query started with `"Where"`, even though the memory had zero content words matching the user's intent (*Kolkovna restaurant* vs. *yoga classes*).
4. **Lexical Asymmetry in Q3:** The proposition *"It's been super helpful for me, especially on days when I can't make it to Serenity Yoga."* scored `relevance = 0.2798` (above the 0.18 threshold) and composite score `0.5175`, but was pushed down to Rank 9 because competing items matched 2 content tokens or received the unanchored slot boost.

### Hypotheses Requiring Validation in Subsequent Batches:
1. **Slot Boost Keyword Anchoring:** Requiring at least one non-stopword content keyword match (`len(intersection) > 0`) before applying a positive `slot_boost` (+0.40) would prevent pure distractor items (like Kolkovna) from leaping over relevant content.
2. **Yoga Studio Entity Association:** Linking "Serenity Yoga" to a location/facility slot or bridging "studio" / "place" during candidate extraction would strengthen the fact's retrieval score.
3. **Top-K Expansion vs. Precision Tradeoff:** Expanding `top_k` from 5 to 10 would capture Q3 at Rank 9, but would reduce Precision@K across the board. Precision and budget tradeoffs should be monitored before adjusting `top_k`.

## Focused Experiment: Slot-Boost Ranking on Question 3 (`6ade9755`)

**Commit:** Experiment following `b2ca25a`  
**Focus:** Slot-boost ranking anomaly where query *"Where do I take yoga classes?"* gave unrelated Prague restaurant (*Kolkovna*) a +0.40 slot boost, ranking it #1.

### 1. Reproduction of Component Scores (Observed Data)
- **Query:** *"Where do I take yoga classes?"*
- **Query Content Words:** `['classes', 'take', 'yoga']` (with `'where'` filtered by `COMMON_STOPWORDS`).
- **Slot Trigger:** `'where'` in query triggered `slot_keywords["location"] = ["live", "located", "city", "reside", "where", "bangalore", "london"]`.
- **Memory:** `*Kolkovna:** This restaurant is located in the heart of Prague and serves traditional Czech cuisine.`
  - `mem_slot`: `"location"` (assigned via regex pattern `located in`).
  - `intersection`: `set()` (0 content words matched).
  - `content_overlap`: `0.0`.
  - `base_sim`: `0.1301`.
  - **Observed Slot Boost:** `+0.40` (Awarded unconditionally via `mem_slot in query_matched_slots`).
  - **Relevance:** `0.65 * 0.0 + 0.35 * 0.1301 + 0.40 = 0.4455`.
  - **Composite Score:** `0.6101` (**Rank 1**).

### 2. Implementation: Anchored Slot Boost
In `memory_retrieval.py` (`compute_query_relevance`):
```python
    base_sim = compute_text_similarity(query, memory.content)

    if mem_slot and mem_slot in query_matched_slots:
        # Require content keyword overlap or substantial text similarity before applying slot boost,
        # preventing unrelated memories with zero query overlap from overriding relevance.
        if len(intersection) > 0 or base_sim >= 0.20:
            slot_boost = 0.40
    elif query_matched_slots and (mem_slot is None or mem_slot not in query_matched_slots):
        if memory.lifecycle_status == LifecycleStatus.TEMPORARY:
            slot_boost = -0.30
```
- **Preservation of Legitimate Location Matches:**
  - Queries such as *"Where do I live?"* with memory *"I live in Bangalore"* share `'live'` (`len(intersection) == 1 > 0`), retaining the `+0.40` boost and full 1.0 relevance.
- **Suppression of Zero-Overlap Distractors:**
  - Unrelated memories with 0 content overlap and `base_sim < 0.20` receive `slot_boost = 0.0`, dropping Kolkovna's relevance from `0.4455` to `0.0455`, falling below `min_relevance_threshold = 0.18`.

## Confirmed Findings vs. Hypotheses

### Confirmed Findings:
1. **Multi-Session Multi-Fact Aggregation:** In single-session questions (Batch 1), retrieving a single proposition achieved Recall=1.0. In multi-session questions (Batch 2), queries require 2 to 5 distinct facts from disparate sessions. When one fact matches strong query keywords while another uses natural paraphrasing, the second fact is often displaced.
2. **Threshold Cutoff Pathology on Multi-Token Queries (Q7):** In queries with 7 or more content words, matching 1 valid keyword (e.g. `December`) yields an overlap ratio of $1/7 = 0.143$, producing relevance $< 0.18$. This causes legitimate memories to be dropped entirely, leading to zero retrieved results for Q7.
3. **Evaluator Recall Counting Artifact (Q10):** In `evaluate_retrieval_quality`, `matches_found` counts retrieved memories rather than unique ground-truth evidence items. When two propositions from the same evidence fact are retrieved, `matches_found` increments to 2, reporting Recall=1.0 even when the second required fact was not retrieved.
4. **Lexical Synonym Gaps (Q6, Q8, Q9):** Common transactional synonyms (`bought` vs `purchased`), entity names without category nouns (`Uber Eats` without `food delivery`), and verb inflections (`working` vs `worked`) create zero keyword intersections.

### Hypotheses for Targeted Improvement (For User Review):
1. **Adaptive Query-Length Relevance Cutoff:** Scaling the minimum relevance threshold dynamically based on query length (e.g. $\min(0.18, 1.2 / |Q_{\text{content}}|)$) would allow Q7's dated December events to qualify without admitting noise.
2. **Purchase Synonym Bridging:** Expanding synonym equivalence to `{bought, purchased, bought, got}` (analogous to the expense synonyms `{cost, fee, price, paid}`) would bridge Q6's scratch grains.
3. **Compound Query Sub-Clause Retrieval / Diversification:** For queries containing compound conjuncts (e.g. *"chicken fajitas AND lentil soup"*), retrieving top items per sub-clause would ensure both topics are represented in the top 5.

## 1. Unique Evidence Counting Evaluator Fix

### Implementation:
In `evaluation_harness.py`, `evaluate_retrieval_quality` was updated to track unique ground-truth evidence coverage:
- Maintains `covered_evidence_indices: Set[int]` across retrieved items.
- **Recall@K** measures the proportion of distinct evidence facts retrieved:  
  $$\text{Recall@K} = \frac{|\text{covered\_evidence\_indices}|}{|\text{supporting\_evidence}|}$$
  Duplicate retrieved propositions matching the same ground-truth turn/fact increment coverage only once.
- **Precision@K** maintains the standard IR definition:  
  $$\text{Precision@K} = \frac{\text{relevant\_retrieved\_count}}{K}$$
- **MRR** remains the reciprocal rank of the first relevant retrieved proposition: $1.0 / \text{first\_hit\_rank}$.
- Empty supporting evidence (`supporting_evidence == []`) returns `NaN` transparently across all metrics and is excluded from aggregate denominator counts.

### Regression Tests Added:

- `test_duplicate_propositions_count_as_single_evidence_coverage`: Verifies that retrieving two chicken fajitas propositions against two ground-truth facts (fajitas + lentil soup) produces $\text{Recall@3} = 1/2 = 0.50$, $\text{Precision@3} = 2/3 = 0.6667$, and $\text{MRR} = 1.0$.
